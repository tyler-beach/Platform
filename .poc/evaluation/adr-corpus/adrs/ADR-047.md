# ADR-047: A transient dependency may be retried no more than five times for a single request

## Status

Accepted for this synthetic evaluation corpus.

## Context

Production services need a consistent control that can be reviewed across repositories and deployment environments. The control applies to production workloads unless a service-specific exception is approved and recorded.

## Decision

A transient dependency may be retried no more than five times for a single request.

## Consequences

Teams must document exceptions with an owner, scope, expiration date, and compensating control. Evidence of configuration alone may not establish deployed or runtime state.
